## Decisions for the owner
| Decision | Trade-off | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Color on by default from the Mac apps | Today the app's dialog wins: the browser sent `ColorModel=Color` and all 14 pages used cyan and magenta toner (cyan 50%, magenta about 250 pages left). Forcing black and white on the Pi would also stop the phone color jobs the owner accepted on 2026-10-22, and nothing like it was designed or tested | Leave as is; choose "Black and white" in the print dialog when color is not needed |
| What to do with `painel` | It restarts every ~5 s, about 2 s of CPU each time, because `/dev/fb0` does not exist; it lives in another repo ([pi-painel](https://github.com/iklobato/pi-painel)). | Power supply of the Pi | `throttled=0x50000` and one under-voltage event at 2026-11-17 13:40:59 point to the supply or cable; on 2026-11-21 `dmesg` held 29 `Undervoltage detected!` lines from 2026-11-20 21:05 to 22:07, after an unexplained reboot at 19:26.
